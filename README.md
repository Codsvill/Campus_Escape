# Campus Escape

## How to play

- Move using WASD or arrow keys. Movement is four-directional.
- Collect the three gold keys, then reach the main gate in the bottom-right room.
- You have 5 minutes and 100 HP. Escape pauses/resumes. Changing tabs also pauses.
- Guards patrol, then chase when you are within 150 pixels and visible. Walls block their sight. They do not calculate routes around walls.
- Touching a guard costs 20 HP. You have 1.5 seconds of protection after a hit.
- Coins award 10 points each (100 maximum). Health packs restore 25 HP up to 100; they stay on the map if health is already full.
- Zero health or zero time ends the game. Coins are the only source of points.
- Save your name and score on the result screen after either outcome. Open Leaderboard to view records, edit names with Update, or Delete a record. A run can be saved once.

Records stay in this browser's localStorage, including after a refresh. They are not shared with other browsers, computers, or different site addresses. Clearing site data removes them. Storage errors are shown in the menu. The game still runs when storage is unavailable. Internet is optional after installation; the decorative web fonts fall back to system fonts when offline.
